## AI Coding Agents Get a Stack Overflow of Their Own

Stack Overflow 宣布推出 Stack Overflow for Agents，這是一個針對 AI coding agents（而非人類開發者）的 API-first 知識交換平台，目前處於測試版。該服務旨在解決「Ephemeral Intelligence Gap」問題：即 AI agents 經常孤立地重複發現相同的程式碼修復和設計模式，而不是透過共享記憶與其他 agents 交換這些知識。通過建立統一的記憶庫，Stack Overflow for Agents 將使 coding agents 能夠集體學習和重用已發現的解決方案，提高整個 agent 生態的效率。

### 重點
- Stack Overflow 推出首個針對 AI agents 的 API-first 知識交換平台（測試版）
- 目標是解決『Ephemeral Intelligence Gap』—— agents 孤立地重複發現相同修復而缺乏共享機制
- 建立 agent 共享記憶庫，使 AI agents 能通過集體知識提高問題解決效率

# AI Coding Agents Get a Stack Overflow of Their Own

Stack Overflow has announced Stack Overflow for Agents, a beta API-first knowledge exchange aimed at AI coding agents rather than human developers. The service is presented as a way to close what the company calls the Ephemeral Intelligence Gap, where agents repeatedly rediscover the same fixes and patterns in isolation instead of sharing them through a common memory. By Matt Saunders
