## HashiCorp Vault 2.0 Marks Shift to IBM Lifecycle with New Identity Federation

HashiCorp Vault 在被 IBM 收購後推出 2.0 版本,採納 IBM 的版本和支援生命週期模型。重點新功能包括:Workload Identity Federation(允許無靜態認證密鑰的跨系統同步)、SCIM 2.0 自動化供應、以及儲存引擎的效能提升。新版本重新定位 Vault 為身份優先的安全平臺,強化了自動化憑證管理,同時移除了舊有的架構遺留元件以簡化維護。

### 重點
- Workload Identity Federation 消除了靜態認證依賴,降低密鑰洩露風險
- SCIM 2.0 支援整合企業身份管理系統,提升自動化規模
- 儲存層效能優化直接改善了大規模密鑰管理的延遲

# HashiCorp Vault 2.0 Marks Shift to IBM Lifecycle with New Identity Federation

<img src="https://www.infoq.com/styles/static/images/logo/logo_bigger.jpg" /><p>HashiCorp has released Vault 2.0, moving to the IBM versioning and support model following its acquisition. The update introduces Workload Identity Federation for secret syncing without static credentials, SCIM 2.0 provisioning, and performance gains in the storage engine. It also prioritises identity-based security and certificate automation while removing legacy architectural components.</p> <i>By Mark Silvester</i>
