## InfoQ Launches Online AI Engineering Cohort and Certification for Senior Software Practitioners

InfoQ 推出 5 週線上 AI Engineering 認證課程，面向生產系統開發的高級從業者。課程涵蓋 RAG、agents、AI platforms、evals、reliability 與操作權衡 (operational trade-offs)。此認證旨在培養能夠在生產環境中構建、部署和運維 AI 系統的專業人才。

### 重點
- 課程週期：5 週線上同步課程，針對 senior software practitioners on production AI systems
- 涵蓋主題：RAG、agents、AI platforms、evals、reliability、operational trade-offs
