## Quoting Andrew Kelley

Zig 創建者 Andrew Kelley 在討論 LLM 輔助程式碼時指出，LLM 生成的錯誤特徵與人類開發者的錯誤在本質上不同，因此相對容易識別。他強調 LLM 輔助開發有明顯的「數字氣味」，對於不使用 LLM 的開發者而言是直覺可察覺的。此觀點反映了開源社群對 AI 輔助貢獻品質的持續關注。

### 重點
- LLM 幻覺錯誤與人類開發者失誤的特徵本質不同，利於識別
- LLM 輔助程式碼有特定的「數字氣味」特徵，對經驗豐富的開發者可直觀感知
- Zig 等嚴格項目對 LLM 輔助貢獻的檢測能力不應被低估

<blockquote cite="https://lobste.rs/s/ifcyr1/contributor_poker_zig_s_ai_ban#c_cbtxub"><p>It's a common misconception that we can't tell who is using LLM and who is not. I'm sure we didn't catch 100% of LLM-assisted PRs over the past few months, but the kind of mistakes humans make are fundamentally different than LLM hallucinations, making them easy to spot. Furthermore, people who come from the world of agentic coding have a certain <em>digital smell</em> that is not obvious to them but is obvious to those who abstain. It's like when a smoker walks into the room, everybody who doesn't smoke instantly knows it.</p>
<p>I'm not telling you not to smoke, but I am telling you not to smoke in my house.</p></blockquote>
