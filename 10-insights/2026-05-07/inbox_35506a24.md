## New Gemma 4 MTP on MLX?

Google 發佈了 Gemma 4 的 Multi Token Prediction (MTP) speculative decoding drafters，可透過輕量級 drafter 模型預測多個 token 並並行驗證，理論上加速推論 2-3 倍。使用者嘗試在 MLX (蘋果機器學習框架) 上整合此功能時遇困，發現 MLX 目前尚不支持 MTP 的實作。

### 重點
- Gemma 4 MTP speculative decoding 可加速推論 2-3×，利用輕量 drafter 並行預測-驗證
- MLX 現階段尚未支持 Gemma 4 MTP，限制了蘋果硬體使用者的整合選項

In case you haven't heard, Google just released Multi Token Prediction drafters for Gemma 4, a speculative decoding approach that pairs the main model with a lightweight drafter. It can predict several tokens ahead and then verify them in parallel, speeding up inference 2-3x faster. Has anyone used this with MLX? I tried to without success. It does not seem to be supported yet. &#32; submitted by &#32; /u/purealgo [link] &#32; [comments]
