## \"Hardware is the only moat\" - Should we buy new hardware now or wait?

作者反思「硬體是唯一護城河」的論點，推論開源模型終將追上閉源，且 LLM 可能存在理論天花板而無法單獨達成 AGI。基於此，frontier lab 焦點已從 AGI 競賽轉向圍繞模型打造產品、爭奪推論市場份額。隨著推論競爭加劇，大廠傾向大規模購買硬體資源以確保效能優勢；與此同時消費 GPU 供應受數據中心優先化而受限，導致價格上漲。作者觀察到持有 NVLink 3090 stack 的使用者目前獲利甚豐，並拋出購買時機的疑問。

### 重點
- 開源模型最終會追上，加上 LLM 的理論天花板論，frontier lab 策略轉向環繞模型建構產品而非追求 AGI
- 硬體成為稀缺資源：大廠爭相大規模採購以維持推論競爭力，消費級 GPU 因製造商優先數據中心而供應緊張、價格上漲
- 持有 NVLink 3090 stack 的使用者從消費 GPU 稀缺性中獲利，突顯硬體投資時機的價值

&quot;Hardware is the only moat&quot;. I read that quote yesterday, and at first, I thought it was just another person trying to sound smart on Twitter. But after the latest Anthropic + xAI developments, I’m starting to believe it. Open source will probably win in the long run, and even xAI seems to have realized that. Based on what we’ve seen over the last couple of months from leading AI researchers, LLMs alone don’t seem capable of reaching AGI. Because of that, most frontier labs now appear to be focusing more on building products around their models and staying competitive rather than pursuing AGI directly. If LLMs really do have a theoretical ceiling, then it’s only a matter of time before open source catches up completely. What we do know is that inference is going to become even more competitive in the near future. Companies will likely start buying even more hardware and compute resources at massive scale to guarantee good performance for increasingly large models. There’s also the trend of consumer hardware becoming even more expensive, since manufacturers are now prioritizing data center demand over consumer GPUs, creating shortages for regular users. We’re already seeing how happy people who bought stacks of 3090s with NVLink support are right now. So, what do you guys think? Should we wait, or should we upgrade ASAP? &#32; submitted by &#32; /u/Alan_Silva_TI [link] &#32; [comments]
