## What do you think Claude is actually better at than other AI tools?

使用者比較 Claude 與其他 AI 工具的相對優勢，並非聲稱 Claude 在所有方面都更強。三大核心優勢：(1) 長文本理解——能掌握草稿、長郵件、會議紀錄的整體結構與邏輯，而非只優化句子片段；(2) 微妙的語調調整——不止「改正式風格」，而是細緻調整如「降低防禦感」「更自然簡潔」且不過度改動原意；(3) 批評式反饋優於直接重寫——詢問「何處重複？」或「本段真正含義？」的價值遠高於要求模型從零重生。使用者保留其他工具用於快速問答、搜尋與腦力激盪，而將 Claude 視為長文編輯的「讀者協助者」而非聊天機器人。

### 重點
- Claude 的長文本優勢在於『理解整體結構』而非『優化句子』，適合處理複雜草稿的全文邏輯
- 微妙語調調整（防禦感、自然度、簡潔度）無需改動原意——這區分了 Claude 與快速工具的應用邊界
- 批評式反饋（指出問題）比生成式重寫更有價值；其他工具仍適合快速 Q&A 與腦力激盪

I don't think Claude is necessarily stronger in all aspects, but among the AI tools I have tried, it does feel obviously different in several places.For me, the most obvious is the long text. If I post a messy draft, a long email, meeting minutes, or an unfinished article, Claude seems to be able to keep up with the whole content rather than just optimize a few sentences. It is more like understanding the structure of the whole draft.I also think it is particularly good at tone adjustment. It's not just a simple requirement of &quot;making it more professional&quot;, but a more subtle adjustment, such as less defensive, less rigid, more natural and more concise, without excessively changing the original meaning. This may be the main reason why I have been using it to edit content.Another point is that it is very useful when I ask Claude to criticize the draft instead of rewriting it directly. For example, ask it &quot;where is it repeated?&quot; Or &quot;What does this paragraph really mean?&quot; It is usually more valuable than simply letting it generate content from scratch.I will still use other AI tools to do quick questions and answers, search-like tasks, or random brainstorming. But if it's a long article writing and editing, Claude is more like a reader who helps me reread the draft, not just a chat robot. &#32; submitted by &#32; /u/Just-Writing1011 [link] &#32; [comments]
