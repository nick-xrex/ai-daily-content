## Anthropic uses Google Forms to collect Claude Design feedback. Using a free tool from 2008.

觀察：Anthropic 使用免費 2008 年 Google Forms 收集 Claude 設計反饋，而非構建客製化反饋入口。發文者從此推導出 2026 年的更廣泛趨勢——「構建 vs 購買」的決策傾向已反轉。許多公司浪費數週開發客製分析儀表板、CRM、反饋入口，卻未先驗證市場需求。論點：若 Anthropic 不需要客製工具，一般公司更不該重新發明 Typeform。暗示：驗證優於完美，簡單工具優於定製複雜度。

### 重點
- Anthropic 選擇 Google Forms 而非客製反饋系統，體現「簡單可驗證 > 品牌體驗」的決策
- 2026 年趨勢反轉：公司應優先用 Typeform、Stripe 等成熟工具而非建內部平臺，避免週級投資在未驗證的需求
- 適用原則：若前沿 AI 公司都不需要，則一般公司無理由投資客製儀表板/門戶

Spotted this while going through some Claude UX feedback links. The company literally building frontier AI, the same one that could have Claude scaffold a custom feedback portal in an afternoon, is using a free Google form from 2008. Meanwhile I keep seeing companies burn weeks building custom internal tools. Custom analytics dashboards. Custom feedback portals. Custom CRMs. All before they've figured out if anyone wants what they're building. The justification is always the same: brand experience, workflow fit, full control. But if Anthropic doesn't need a bespoke feedback tool, you probably don't need to rebuild Typeform.
