## Speech translation in Google Meet is now rolling out to mobile devices

Google Meet 語音翻譯功能正式推送至行動設備，使用者可在會議中即時實現雙語溝通。該功能目前支援英語、西班牙語、法語、德語、葡萄牙語和義大利語六種語言，採用科幻式翻譯邏輯：兩人用不同語言交談，系統自動翻譯後以類似原說話者的語音重複播放（有短暫延遲）。功能仍處 alpha 階段，在網頁瀏覽器上運作尚可，但在 iPhone 和 iPad 等行動裝置間測試時出現不穩定。

### 重點
- 支援 6 種語言：英、西、法、德、葡、義
- 採用近似原說話者的語音合成重複翻譯內容
- 網頁版可用，行動版體驗不穩定（alpha 階段）

<p><strong><a href="https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/04/speech-translation-in-google-meet-is-now-rolling-out-to-mobile-devices.html">Speech translation in Google Meet is now rolling out to mobile devices</a></strong></p>
I just encountered this feature via a "try this out now" prompt in a Google Meet meeting. It kind-of worked!</p>
<p>This is Google's implementation of the ultimate sci-fi translation app, where two people can talk to each other in two separate languages and Meet translates from one to the other and - with a short delay - repeats the text in your preferred language, with a rough imitation of the original speaker's voice.</p>
<p>It can only handle English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Italian at the moment. It's also still very alpha - I ran it successfully between two laptops running web browsers, but then when I tried between an iPhone and an iPad it didn't seem to work.
