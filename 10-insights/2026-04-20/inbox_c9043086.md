## The 7 Database Mistakes That Quietly Kill Performance (And Nobody Notices Until It’s Too Late)

資料庫性能隱形殺手：7 個常見錯誤往往不在 staging 出現，只在生產暴露導致重損。(1)連線池枯竭（連線未釋放導致新請求等待）曾損失 $18K/2 小時，(2)大表 OFFSET 分頁掃描 100K+ 列造成 CPU 尖峰，(3)外鍵缺少索引導致 JOIN 掃全表，(4)SELECT * 在列數變動時成本翻倍，(5)無監控工具（應用 pg_stat_statements 識別最耗時查詢），(6)長事務持鎖導致寫入阻塞，(7)無背壓機制持續堆積請求。一個具體案例：2M 列表使用 OFFSET 分頁損失 $47K/日，改用遊標式分頁立即 10 倍加速。

### 重點
- 連線池洩漏是隱形癱瘓根因，try-with-resources 一行代碼即可解決；OFFSET 分頁在百萬級列數下每次掃描 100 倍數據，遊標式分頁可 10 倍加速
- 外鍵無索引、SELECT * 動態列數變化、無即時查詢監控是 production 隱形成本；應用 pg_stat_statements 可識別排名前 10 最耗時查詢
- 7 個錯誤都可監測可修復：連線池、分頁策略、索引覆蓋、欄位選擇、監控工具、事務範圍、背壓限制

# The 7 Database Mistakes That Quietly Kill Performance (And Nobody Notices Until It’s Too Late)

<h4>It’s not your query. It’s everything around it slowly setting your system on fire.</h4><figure><img alt="" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/976/1*jMhCRlo8RusvCSLU8Kkozg.png" /></figure><p>Last Tuesday, a perfectly “optimized” query brought a fintech app to its knees.</p><p>It wasn’t slow in staging. It wasn’t slow in testing.<br />But in production?</p><p><strong>Everything froze.</strong></p><h3>The Problem Isn’t Where You Think</h3><p>Here’s the uncomfortable truth:</p><p><strong>Most slow systems don’t have slow queries. They have slow habits.</strong></p><p>Developers love blaming SQL.<br />But in real production incidents, the root cause is usually something else entirely.</p><p>Something subtle.<br />Something boring.<br />Something nobody checks.</p><p>Let’s break down the 7 mistakes I keep seeing over and over again.</p><h3>1. Connection Pool Exhaustion (The Silent Killer)</h3><p>Your database is fine.<br />Your queries are fine.</p><p>But your app? It’s stuck waiting.</p><p>Because all connections are… gone.</p><p><strong>What happens:</strong></p><ul><li>Each request grabs a DB connection</li><li>Some never release it (thanks, timeouts)</li><li>Pool fills up</li><li>New requests wait → then fail</li></ul><p><strong>Real impact:</strong></p><p><strong>$18,000 lost in 2 hours. Just because connections weren’t returned.</strong></p><p><strong>Typical mistake:</strong></p><pre>Connection conn = dataSource.getConnection();<br />// do work<br />// forgot to close</pre><p><strong>Fix:</strong></p><pre>try (Connection conn = dataSource.getConnection()) {<br />    // do work<br />}</pre><p>One line.<br />That’s the difference between uptime and chaos.</p><h3>2. OFFSET Pagination on Large Tables</h3><p>Looks harmless. It’s not.</p><pre>SELECT * FROM orders<br />ORDER BY created_at DESC<br />LIMIT 20 OFFSET 100000;</pre><p><strong>What your DB actually does:</strong></p><ul><li>Scans 100,020 rows</li><li>Throws away 100,000</li><li>Returns 20</li></ul><p>Every. Single. Time.</p><p><strong>It gets slower the more your app grows.</strong></p><p><strong>Fix: keyset pagination</strong></p><pre>SELECT * FROM orders<br />WHERE created_at &lt; '2024-01-01'<br />ORDER BY created_at DESC<br />LIMIT 20;</pre><p>Same result.<br /><strong>10x faster.</strong></p><h3>3. Missing Index on Foreign Keys</h3><p>You won’t notice this in small datasets.</p><p>Then one day…</p><p>Your JOINs explode.</p><p><strong>Why it hurts:</strong></p><ul><li>DB scans entire table for each join</li><li>CPU spikes</li><li>Queries time out</li></ul><p><strong>Example:</strong></p><pre>SELECT * FROM orders o<br />JOIN users u ON o.user_id = u.id;</pre><p>If orders.user_id is not indexed?</p><p>Good luck.</p><h3>4. SELECT * in Production</h3><p>This one is everywhere.</p><pre>SELECT * FROM users;</pre><p>Looks innocent.</p><p>But in production:</p><ul><li>You fetch unnecessary columns</li><li>You increase memory usage</li><li>You slow down network transfer</li></ul><p><strong>Especially deadly when tables evolve.</strong></p><p>Today: 5 columns<br />6 months later: 35 columns + JSON blobs</p><p>Same query.<br />Very different cost.</p><h3>5. No Query Monitoring (Flying Blind)</h3><p>Most teams don’t know:</p><ul><li>Which queries are slow</li><li>Which queries run most</li><li>Which queries are killing them</li></ul><p>They guess.</p><p><strong>Guessing in production is expensive.</strong></p><p>One thing that changed everything for me:</p><pre>SELECT * FROM pg_stat_statements<br />ORDER BY total_time DESC<br />LIMIT 10;</pre><p>That one query shows:</p><ul><li>Your worst offenders</li><li>Where to focus</li><li>What actually matters</li></ul><h3>6. Long Transactions (The Hidden Lock Problem)</h3><p>Everything looks fine… until it’s not.</p><p>Then suddenly:</p><ul><li>Inserts block</li><li>Updates freeze</li><li>Users complain</li></ul><p>Because one transaction is holding locks too long.</p><p><strong>Typical cause:</strong></p><ul><li>Doing too much inside a transaction</li><li>Waiting on external services</li></ul><p><strong>Example mistake:</strong></p><pre>@Transactional<br />public void processOrder() {<br />    saveOrder();<br />    callExternalPaymentAPI(); // 🚨<br />}</pre><p>If that API is slow?</p><p>Your DB is locked the entire time.</p><h3>7. Ignoring Database Backpressure</h3><p>This is where things get dangerous.</p><p>Your app keeps sending requests.<br />Your DB keeps slowing down.</p><p>No one stops.</p><p>No one limits.</p><p>No one adapts.</p><p>Until…</p><p><strong>Everything crashes at once.</strong></p><h3>One Real Incident (And Why It Matters)</h3><p>One of the incidents I documented:</p><p>A team used OFFSET pagination on a table with 2 million rows.<br />Traffic increased. Queries slowed. CPU hit 100%.</p><p><strong>Result: $47K lost in a single day.</strong></p><p>The fix?</p><p>Switch to cursor-based pagination.</p><p>That’s it.</p><p>I broke down 10+ real database failures like this — with exact queries, fixes, and detection steps →</p><p><a href="https://yusufseyitoglu.gumroad.com/l/db-incident-playbook">Your Database Is Bleeding Money. The Incident Playbook.</a></p><p>Why This Matters More Than You Think</p><p>Here’s the scary part:</p><p>These issues don’t show up immediately.</p><p>They grow silently.</p><ul><li>Your dataset grows</li><li>Your traffic increases</li><li>Your latency creeps up</li></ul><p>Then one day…</p><p><strong>Everything breaks at once.</strong></p><p>And you don’t have time to “learn databases” anymore.</p><p>You’re firefighting.</p><h3>What You Should Do Instead</h3><p>Forget “perfect queries.”</p><p>Focus on these:</p><ul><li><strong>Monitor first, optimize second</strong></li><li><strong>Design for growth, not today’s data</strong></li><li><strong>Assume every query will run 1M times</strong></li><li><strong>Fail fast instead of piling requests</strong></li></ul><p>And most importantly:</p><p><strong>The database is not just a storage layer. It’s your system’s heartbeat.</strong></p><p>Treat it like one.</p><h3>The Takeaway Nobody Tells You</h3><p>You don’t get paid for writing fast queries.</p><p>You get paid for <strong>preventing slow systems.</strong></p><p>That’s a completely different skill.</p><p>And most developers never learn it… until production teaches them the hard way.</p><img alt="" height="1" src="https://medium.com/_/stat?event=post.clientViewed&amp;referrerSource=full_rss&amp;postId=e5045bb94184" width="1" /><hr /><p><a href="https://blog.stackademic.com/the-7-database-mistakes-that-quietly-kill-performance-and-nobody-notices-until-its-too-late-e5045bb94184">The 7 Database Mistakes That Quietly Kill Performance (And Nobody Notices Until It’s Too Late)</a> was originally published in <a href="https://blog.stackademic.com">Stackademic</a> on Medium, where people are continuing the conversation by highlighting and responding to this story.</p>
