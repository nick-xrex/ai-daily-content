# Computer-use MCP that can control multiple machines (Integrate with claude, Cursor, Codex or your custom harness)

Hey everyone, We built opendesk: it lets AI agents control your desktop using computer use MCP that can integrate with your custom workflow. Today we shipped something a bit wild: Your AI can now see, click, type, and navigate on a completely different computer, over your WiFi. You can pair them once and your agent can control it all from a single conversation. No cloud, account login, or servers in the middle. Everything stays on your local network, fully encrypted.
