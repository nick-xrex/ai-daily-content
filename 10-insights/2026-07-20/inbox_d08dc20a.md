## Quoting Sam Altman

2022 年 10 月，Sam Altman 在寄給 OpenAI 董事會的信件中提出，OpenAI 計畫開發一款約當 GPT-3 能力的語言模型，能在消費級硬體上本地運行。他強調要「盡快」發布這個模型，搶在 Stability AI 或其他競爭者之前。Altman 明確表示，這個策略的核心目的是「阻止他人發行類似強大的開源模型，並讓新興 AI 創業公司更難獲得融資」。這份信件近日在 2026 年的 Musk v. Altman 訴訟中首次曝光，直接揭露了 OpenAI 對開源語言模型戰略的真實競爭動機——而不是單純的「民主化 AI」名義。

### 重點
- OpenAI 計畫在消費級硬體上本地部署 GPT-3 級別語言模型（2022 年計畫）
- 明確的競爭目標：阻止 Stability 等對手發布同等級開源模型、抬高新創融資難度
- 2026 年訴訟首次曝光信件，揭露業界龍頭對開源戰略的市場抑制動機

# Quoting Sam Altman

We have been having extensive discussions around open source strategy. We will discuss it more at our next board meeting, but one thing we’d like to do soon is to create a language model with the approximate capability of GPT-3 that can run locally on consumer hardware and release that. We’d like to do it soon, before Stability or someone else does. In general, we think this helps discourage others from releasing similarly-powerful models, and makes it harder for new efforts to get funded. 
 &mdash; Sam Altman , Email to OpenAI's board, October 1, 2022 - exposed in Musk v. Altman (2026)
