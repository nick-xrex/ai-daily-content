## Podcast: WebAssembly on the JVM: Feature Evolution, Performance, and the Transition to Endive

播客討論 WebAssembly 在 JVM 伺服器端應用及性能演進。Andrea Peruffo 介紹 Wasm 從瀏覽器技術走向邊緣計算和後端系統。核心效能進步是從解釋器執行轉向 JIT 編譯，顯著提升運行效率。實際應用涵蓋邊緣計算平台和模組化外掛架構，展示 Wasm 作為隔離、可靠計算層的價值。播客涉及向「Endive」的過渡，暗示 Wasm 運行時生態的重大演進。

### 重點
- JIT 編譯相比解釋器帶來數倍性能提升，使 Wasm 在伺服器端可行
- Wasm 用於外掛和邊緣計算的隔離性和可攜性是核心優勢
- Endive 過渡標誌 Wasm 運行時標準化和成熟化的里程碑

# Podcast: WebAssembly on the JVM: Feature Evolution, Performance, and the Transition to Endive

Andrea Peruffo discusses the evolution of WebAssembly beyond the browser and its growing role on the server-side JVM. He covers performance advancements in Wasm runtimes, moving from interpreters to efficient JIT compilation, and explores real-world production use cases ranging from edge computing platforms to modular plugin architectures. By Andrea Peruffo
